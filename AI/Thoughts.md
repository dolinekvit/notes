## My thoughts on AI so far
- AI can be useful or harmful depending on the use case.
- - On one side it makes my life easier if it is helping me with functionality and architecture, however, I can quickly lose track of what is going on in the repository.
- - Additionally, it dulls common sense of a programmer. Rather than doing hard programmer work, it is easy to get used to AI being there for you. What if the tokens run out, though?

## My AI usage -- Retrospectively
- I have started around 2023-2024 ish with Copilot where the copilot would autocomplete as I was typing. It seemed cool at first but it can be quite nuisance and distracting. 
- - Chat mode was a bit better although there were models that weren't that capable at the time.
- From Copilot to Cursor, it was yet another clone of VSCode and the usage was awful. I reckon it might be more polished now.
- Cursor to Codex - it was more or less required to use it where I was working. It was sort of neat when u included all the skills but one bad prompt and there goes your work. 
- - I used to say I'm professional reader cuz I was just reading specs and implementation documents while not doing any coding myself. It is kind of extreme and most of the management might say it's where we are heading.
- - - My personal opinion is that there will always be the need to write the code and AI will act as a productivity tool rather then 'do everything for me' kind of tool. 

The usage of AI is not bad, I think. It all depends on how you use it. The biggest pain points are losing track of what you are doing, joy in what you do. I wonder if the AI got ridiculously expensive, how many programmers would be able to keep doing the craft.
